# Clinical Foundations — Discipline Pillars for a South African State-Hospital Intern-Support App

> Purpose: define the *non-negotiable* clinical scaffolding of each department so the app can (a) prompt interns for the history/exam items consultants always expect, (b) structurally support the charts and scores each discipline lives by, and (c) trend serial investigations one step ahead of the intern.
> Context: South African public sector, intern + community-service level, resource-variable (district → tertiary).
> Convention: numbers/thresholds are given where a source states them. Items marked **[EK]** rely on established knowledge because the web source was unreachable or the point is textbook-standard.

### Sources actually consulted (see per-section citations and final report)
- SA National Dept of Health (NDoH) **Adult Hospital Level STGs & EML** — chapters updated 2020–2024 (e.g. HIV/AIDS chapter v2.1, Dec 2024) — health.gov.za / Knowledge Hub.
- NDoH **Hospital Level (Paediatric) STGs & EML, 5th edition, 2023** — Knowledge Hub / paediatrics.org.za.
- NDoH **Primary Healthcare STGs & EML, 7th edition 2020** + **Chapter 13 Immunisation, Version 1.1, 6 Dec 2024**.
- NDoH **Guidelines for Maternity Care in South Africa, 4th edition** + **National Integrated Maternal, Perinatal & Neonatal (MPN) Guidelines 2023**.
- NDoH **2023 ART Clinical Guidelines** (adults, pregnancy/breastfeeding, adolescents, children, infants, neonates) — TLD first-line.
- NDoH / NICD **Management of Drug-Resistant TB: Policy Guidelines 2023** (BPaL/BPaLM era).
- **South African Triage Scale (SATS)** incl. TEWS (adult + paediatric), Western Cape EMS / Emergency Medicine Society of SA.
- **Mental Health Care Act 17 of 2002** and regulations (MHCA forms 04–11).
- Global: WHO guidance, ATLS 10th ed conventions, ACLS/AHA, Surviving Sepsis Campaign 2021, ARDSNet/PROSEVA, WHO Surgical Safety Checklist, RCOG/ACOG, Oxford Handbook-style frameworks — several **[EK]** where not directly re-fetched this session.

## Internal Medicine
*Sources: Adult Hospital Level STGs & EML (NDoH, chapters 2020–2024); 2023 ART Clinical Guidelines; NICD DR-TB 2023; Oxford Handbook of Clinical Medicine framework [EK]; Surviving Sepsis 2021 [EK].*

### Non-negotiable history pillars
- Presenting complaint with **timeline** (onset, tempo, progression) — acute vs subacute vs chronic reframes the whole differential.
- Full **systems review** — cardio-resp, GIT, GU, neuro, constitutional (weight loss, night sweats, fevers = TB/HIV/malignancy triad).
- **HIV status and date of last test**; if positive: last CD4, last viral load, current ART regimen + start date + adherence, prior OIs. (2023 ART: default TLD.)
- **TB history**: previous TB, treatment completion, current cough/weight loss/night sweats, contacts, DR-TB risk.
- **Chronic disease control**: HTN, DM (last HbA1c), CKD, IHD, CCF, epilepsy, asthma/COPD — and *actual adherence*, not the prescription.
- **Full drug history incl. traditional/OTC** — nephrotoxins (NSAIDs, aminoglycosides), traditional medicines (AKI, hepatotoxicity), warfarin/DOAC.
- **Allergies** (and what the reaction actually was).
- **Substance use**: alcohol (quantify — CIWA risk), smoking pack-years, other.
- **Functional baseline & social**: who cares for the patient, stairs, employment, grant status — drives disposition.

### Examination routine (order actually performed)
1. End-of-bed **gestalt**: sick/not sick, respiratory distress, cachexia, level of consciousness.
2. **Vitals + GCS + glucose (HGT)** — always a finger-prick in altered mental status.
3. **General/hands**: clubbing, koilonychia, pallor, jaundice, oedema, lymphadenopathy, hydration, oral thrush (HIV clue).
4. **CVS**: pulse, JVP, apex, heart sounds, murmurs, peripheral oedema.
5. **Respiratory**: RR, chest expansion, percussion, auscultation, effusion signs.
6. **Abdomen**: distension, tenderness, organomegaly, ascites (shifting dullness), PR when indicated.
7. **Neuro**: GCS, cranial nerves, tone/power/reflexes/sensation, meningism.
8. Targeted: joints, skin, breast, fundoscopy (DM/HTN/raised ICP).

### Investigations TRACKED over time — and what the trend means
- **UEC daily in AKI** — creatinine trajectory *is* the decision: rising despite fluids/stopping nephrotoxins → nephrology/dialysis conversation; falling → recovery. Track urine output (mL/kg/h) alongside.
- **K⁺** — trend against drug chart (ACEi/ARB, spironolactone, trimethoprim); rise >0.5–0.7 mmol/L/24h or absolute >6.0 → ECG + emergency Rx.
- **CRP vs ESR trajectory** — falling CRP = responding infection; persistently high/rising = source control failure. ESR high + normal CRP leans inflammatory/autoimmune.
- **FBC**: Hb trend (bleeding vs marrow vs haemolysis); WCC trend (sepsis response); platelet trend (sepsis/DIC/drug).
- **Glucose/HbA1c**: HbA1c for chronic control; capillary glucose trend for inpatient titration.
- **LFTs** — pattern (hepatocellular ALT/AST vs cholestatic ALP/GGT) tracked on TB/ART drugs (DILI). Bilirubin + INR = synthetic function.
- **CD4 + viral load** over months — VL suppression <50 by 3–6 months; failure to suppress → adherence vs resistance.
- **Lactate** — clearance marker in sepsis; failure to clear = ongoing hypoperfusion.
- **INR** on warfarin; **troponin** serial in ACS.

### Classic omissions that cause harm
- Not checking **glucose** in altered mental status → missed hypoglycaemia (reversible in seconds). 
- Not offering **HIV test** → missed OI, missed TB co-infection, wrong differential.
- Forgetting **VTE prophylaxis** in the immobile medical patient → PE.
- Not reviewing the **drug chart against renal function** → accumulating nephrotoxins/renally-cleared drugs.
- Missing **hyperkalaemia ECG** before it becomes an arrest.
- Not recognising **sepsis** early — no lactate, delayed antibiotics (target <1h).
- Omitting **thiamine before glucose** in alcohol-dependent (Wernicke's).
- Not documenting **baseline neuro** before sedation/opioids.

### Scoring systems / charts to structurally support
- **CURB-65** (pneumonia severity/disposition), **qSOFA / SOFA** (sepsis), **NEWS/TEWS** vitals track.
- **GCS** (component breakdown E/V/M).
- **Wells score** (DVT & PE) + PERC.
- **CHA₂DS₂-VASc** + **HAS-BLED** (AF anticoagulation).
- **CIWA-Ar** (alcohol withdrawal), **Glasgow-Blatchford / Rockall** (UGIB).
- **CKD staging (eGFR/ACR)**, **Child-Pugh / MELD** (liver).
- **Diabetic emergency charts** (DKA/HHS fluid + insulin + K⁺ tracking).

### SA-specific must-knows
- **TB/HIV integration**: every HIV+ screened for TB at each visit (symptom screen + investigations); TPT (TB preventive therapy) if no active TB. Start ART timing relative to TB (and CD4 <50 → within 2 weeks; TB meningitis delay).
- **TLD** (tenofovir-lamivudine-dolutegravir) is default first-line ART (2023 guideline); switch non-DTG patients.
- **DR-TB**: BPaL/BPaLM era regimens (NICD 2023) — baseline ECG (QT), audiometry historically, LFTs.
- **Notifiable medical conditions (NMC)**: TB, measles, meningococcal disease, viral haemorrhagic fevers, cholera, food-borne outbreaks, etc. — notify via NMC system.
- **CD4 <200** → cotrimoxazole prophylaxis; **CrAg screening** if CD4 <100/200.

## Surgery (General)
*Sources: Adult Hospital Level STGs & EML (NDoH); WHO Surgical Safety Checklist; ATLS 10th ed [EK]; Oxford Handbook of Clinical Surgery [EK].*

### Non-negotiable history pillars
- **Indication for surgery** — stated explicitly, with the diagnosis it rests on.
- **Previous operations + complications** (bleeding, infection, anaesthetic problems, adhesions).
- **NPO / fasting status** (last solid and clear fluids — timing).
- **Anaesthetic history** (see Anaesthetics dept — reactions, difficult airway, PONV, family MH).
- **Comorbidities that change risk**: IHD, diabetes, COPD, CKD, cirrhosis, bleeding disorders.
- **Anticoagulants / antiplatelets** — last dose, indication (mechanical valve vs AF).
- **Bleeding history** and family bleeding tendency.
- **Bowel history** for GIT: change in habit, PR bleeding, weight loss, obstruction symptoms.
- **HIV/TB status** (wound healing, abdominal TB), **allergies**, **smoking/alcohol**.

### Examination routine (order actually performed)
1. **General/ABC + vitals** (shock in acute abdomen/trauma).
2. **Hydration/perfusion** — cap refill, mucous membranes, urine output.
3. **Abdominal exam**: inspection (distension, scars, hernias), auscultation (bowel sounds — before palpation), percussion (tympany/dullness/ascites), palpation (guarding, rebound, masses, organomegaly), **hernial orifices + external genitalia**.
4. **PR examination** (masses, blood, prostate, tone) — the finger you don't put in…
5. **Wound/stoma review** post-op: erythema, discharge, dehiscence; **drain sites + outputs**.
6. Focused: neck/thyroid, breast, vascular (pulses, ABPI), perianal.

### Investigations TRACKED over time — and what the trend means
- **Post-op Hb drift** — falling Hb = ongoing bleeding vs dilution; serial Hb + haemodynamics decide return-to-theatre.
- **Drain outputs** — volume + character trend (serous → sanguineous → bilious/faeculent = anastomotic leak); sudden rise or change = alarm.
- **WCC + CRP trend post-op** — CRP normally peaks ~day 2–3 then falls; **rising CRP after day 4 = complication (collection/leak/infection)**.
- **Lactate** in acute abdomen/ischaemic bowel — rising = worsening perfusion/ischaemia.
- **UEC + fluid balance** — third-space losses, NG output, AKI.
- **LFTs/amylase-lipase** in pancreatico-biliary disease.
- **ABG/base deficit** in resuscitation of the acute abdomen/trauma.

### Classic omissions that cause harm
- Not documenting/reassessing **NPO status** → aspiration, cancelled list.
- Missing an **incarcerated hernia** or not examining hernial orifices in bowel obstruction.
- Not doing the **PR** → missed rectal cancer, missed GI bleed source.
- Forgetting **VTE prophylaxis + mechanical** peri-operatively.
- Not charting/acting on **drain output changes** (leak recognised late).
- Under-resuscitating before theatre (no lactate, no urine output target).
- Not stopping/bridging **anticoagulation** appropriately.
- Missing **surgical sepsis** source control (drainage) while escalating antibiotics only.
- Not marking the surgical **site/side** and skipping the **WHO checklist**.

### Scoring systems / charts to structurally support
- **WHO Surgical Safety Checklist** (Sign in / Time out / Sign out).
- **ASA physical status** (I–VI) — see Anaesthetics.
- **Glasgow-Blatchford / Rockall** (UGIB), **Alvarado** (appendicitis).
- **Fluid balance chart** (ins/outs, drains, NG, stoma, urine).
- **Post-op observation/early-warning chart (TEWS/NEWS)**.
- **Wound assessment / pressure-injury (Braden)**.

### SA-specific must-knows
- High burden of **trauma (penetrating)** and **abdominal TB** — consider TB in unexplained ascites/masses.
- **HIV** affects wound healing and presentation of intra-abdominal sepsis.
- **J88 medico-legal form** for assault/GBV/gunshot/stab injuries — accurate injury documentation is a legal obligation.
- **Blood product** availability variable — group-and-save early, judicious use, massive transfusion protocol awareness.
- Notify relevant **NMCs** (e.g. tetanus-prone wounds — tetanus prophylaxis per immunisation status).

## Orthopaedics
*Sources: Adult Hospital Level STGs & EML (NDoH — trauma/musculoskeletal); ATLS 10th ed (open fractures, C-spine) [EK]; Oxford Handbook of Orthopaedics & Trauma [EK].*

### Non-negotiable history pillars
- **Mechanism of injury** (energy, direction, position) — predicts fracture pattern and associated injuries.
- **Time of injury** (relevant to open-fracture antibiotic window, compartment syndrome, ischaemia time).
- **Neurovascular symptoms** distal to injury (numbness, pallor, pain out of proportion).
- **Hand dominance / occupation / function** — recovery goals.
- **Open vs closed** wound, contamination, environment.
- **Tetanus immunisation status**.
- **Comorbidities**: diabetes (foot/infection), osteoporosis, RA, PVD, smoking (non-union).
- **Anticoagulants**, **allergies**, **NPO status** (likely theatre).
- In children: **non-accidental injury** red flags (mechanism inconsistent with injury/age).

### Examination routine (order actually performed)
1. **ATLS primary survey first** if trauma (fractures don't kill first).
2. **Look** — deformity, swelling, wounds, skin tenting, shortening/rotation.
3. **Feel** — tenderness, temperature, **distal pulses + capillary refill**, sensation.
4. **Move** — active then passive (only if safe); joint above and below.
5. **Neurovascular status documented before and after any manipulation/splint** — mandatory.
6. **Compartments** — tense, pain on passive stretch (the 5 Ps are late).
7. Specific tests (e.g. anterior drawer, Thompson's for Achilles).

### Investigations TRACKED over time — and what the trend means
- **Serial neurovascular checks** — the "investigation" in a limb at compartment/ischaemia risk; deterioration = emergency fasciotomy/revascularisation.
- **X-ray joint above and below** — post-reduction/post-fixation films to confirm alignment; serial films for **fracture healing / callus / displacement**.
- **Hb** post-op (arthroplasty, pelvic/femoral fractures — significant occult loss).
- **CRP/WCC/ESR** trend in **osteomyelitis / septic arthritis / prosthetic joint infection** — falling = responding.
- **Creatine kinase (CK)** + renal function + urine myoglobin in crush/compartment (rhabdomyolysis).
- **Glucose** in diabetic foot sepsis.

### Classic omissions that cause harm
- **Not documenting neurovascular status** pre- and post-reduction → medico-legal disaster + missed nerve/vessel injury.
- Missing **compartment syndrome** (relying on late signs, not pain-out-of-proportion).
- Missing the **second fracture** / associated injury (e.g. calcaneal + spine; femur + hip).
- Not treating **open fracture** urgently (antibiotics ideally within 1h, tetanus, debridement).
- Not aspirating a hot joint → missed **septic arthritis** (joint destroyed in hours).
- Splint/cast too tight → ischaemia.
- Forgetting **VTE prophylaxis** in the fractured/immobilised patient.
- Missing **NAI** in paediatric fractures.

### Scoring systems / charts to structurally support
- **Gustilo-Anderson** open fracture classification.
- **Neurovascular observation chart** (serial, timed).
- **Compartment pressure** thresholds (ΔP <30 mmHg = fasciotomy).
- **Ottawa ankle/knee rules** (imaging decision).
- **GCS + C-spine clearance (NEXUS / Canadian C-spine)** in trauma.
- **AO/OTA fracture classification**, **Garden** (NOF), **Salter-Harris** (paediatric physeal).

### SA-specific must-knows
- High **penetrating trauma + MVC/pedestrian** burden.
- **TB spine (Pott's disease)** and TB of joints — consider in chronic back pain + constitutional symptoms.
- **HIV**: septic arthritis/osteomyelitis, altered healing.
- **J88** for assault/GSW/stab.
- Tetanus prophylaxis per **EPI** status.

## Obstetrics & Gynaecology
*Sources: Guidelines for Maternity Care in South Africa (4th ed) + National Integrated MPN Guidelines 2023; BANC-Plus (Basic Antenatal Care Plus); ESMOE (Essential Steps in Managing Obstetric Emergencies); 2023 ART Guidelines (PMTCT); RCOG/ACOG [EK].*

### Non-negotiable history pillars — ANTENATAL
- **Gravidity & parity** (+ living children, losses, gestations).
- **LMP / EDD / gestational age** (confirmed by early scan if available); reliability of dates.
- **Previous pregnancies + outcomes + complications**: mode of delivery, C/S indication + type of scar, PPH, PET/eclampsia, stillbirth, prematurity, GDM, birth weights.
- **HIV status + ART** (PMTCT — VL, regimen), **syphilis (RPR)**, **Rh/blood group**, **Hb**.
- **Medical history**: HTN, DM, cardiac, epilepsy, TB, thyroid, mental health.
- **Current pregnancy**: fetal movements, bleeding, PV loss, contractions, symptoms of PET (headache, visual, epigastric pain), danger signs.
- **BANC-Plus visit schedule** adherence.

### Non-negotiable history pillars — LABOUR
- Onset/frequency/strength of **contractions**, **membrane status** (SROM time/colour — meconium), **PV bleeding**, **fetal movements**, GA, parity, previous C/S, HIV/ART/VL, blood group, last meal.

### Non-negotiable history pillars — POSTNATAL
- Delivery details (mode, EBL, complications, 3rd stage), **PPH**, perineal trauma/repair, **lochia**, breastfeeding, bladder/bowel, mood (EPDS), VTE risk, contraception plan, infant feeding + PMTCT (NVP/AZT).

### Non-negotiable history pillars — GYNAE
- **Menstrual history** (cycle, LMP, menorrhagia, IMB/PCB), **contraception**, **pregnancy possibility (always βhCG)**, pain, discharge, **sexual + STI history**, **cervical screening (Pap) history**, prior surgery, HIV status.

### Examination routine (order actually performed)
- **Antenatal**: BP + **urine dipstick (protein)** every visit; weight; pallor/oedema; **SFH (symphysis-fundal height)**; **lie/presentation**; **fetal heart** (Doppler/Pinard); fetal movements.
- **Labour**: maternal vitals, contractions (frequency/duration per 10 min), **abdominal palpation** (lie, presentation, descent in fifths), **fetal heart rate** (intermittent auscultation/CTG), **vaginal exam** (dilatation, effacement, station, membranes, liquor, presenting part/position) → plot **partogram**.
- **Postnatal**: vitals, pallor, uterine tone/height, lochia, perineum/wound, calves (VTE), breasts, mood.
- **Gynae**: abdomen, then **speculum** (cervix, discharge, bleeding, Pap), then **bimanual** (uterine size, adnexal masses/tenderness, cervical excitation).

### Investigations TRACKED over time — and what the trend means
- **BP + urine protein trend** = pre-eclampsia surveillance; rising BP + new proteinuria + symptoms → severe PET/imminent eclampsia.
- **Hb** — antenatal anaemia and especially **post-PPH Hb trend** (guides transfusion, iron).
- **Platelets + LFTs + creatinine + LDH** in **PET/HELLP** — falling platelets, rising ALT/AST/LDH, rising creatinine = HELLP/organ injury = deliver.
- **βhCG serial (48h doubling)** — in early pregnancy/ectopic: <66% rise or plateau suggests ectopic/non-viable; used to track medical management of ectopic (methotrexate) and molar pregnancy resolution.
- **HIV viral load** in pregnancy (PMTCT) — unsuppressed near term escalates infant prophylaxis + mode of delivery discussion.
- **Partogram** — cervimetry vs alert/action lines; **FHR trend**; liquor.
- **Serial CTG** — decelerations/variability trend.

### Classic omissions that cause harm
- **Not doing βhCG** in any woman of reproductive age with abdominal pain/bleeding → missed ectopic (a killer).
- Not checking **BP + urine protein at every ANC visit** → missed pre-eclampsia.
- Not plotting the **partogram** / missing the action line → obstructed labour, ruptured uterus.
- Missing **PPH** quantification (underestimating EBL) and not calling early.
- Not giving **anti-D** to Rh-negative women (sensitising events + routine).
- Forgetting **magnesium sulphate** in severe PET/eclampsia (and its monitoring — reflexes, RR, urine output).
- Not screening/treating **syphilis (RPR)** and not documenting **HIV/PMTCT** plan.
- Not assessing **fetal movements** / acting on reduced movements.
- Postnatal: missing **VTE**, missing **postnatal depression (EPDS)**.
- Not doing **active management of 3rd stage** (uterotonic).

### Scoring systems / charts to structurally support
- **Partogram** (cervimetry, alert/action lines, descent, contractions, FHR, liquor, maternal vitals) — central obstetric chart.
- **Bishop score** (induction favourability).
- **APGAR** (see Paeds/Neonatal).
- **EPDS** (Edinburgh Postnatal Depression Scale) — antenatal + postnatal mood.
- **Modified obstetric early warning score (MEOWS)**.
- **PET/eclampsia MgSO₄ monitoring chart** (reflexes, RR ≥12, urine output ≥0.5 mL/kg/h).
- **BANC-Plus antenatal card / MPN maternal record**.

### SA-specific must-knows
- **BANC-Plus** contact schedule (min 8 contacts) — booking as early as possible; first-visit bloods (HIV, RPR, Hb, blood group, urine).
- **PMTCT** (2023 ART): mother on TLD; infant NVP ± AZT per risk; maternal VL near delivery; test infant (birth PCR, 10 weeks, etc.).
- **ESMOE** emergency drills (PPH, eclampsia, shoulder dystocia, cord prolapse).
- **Confidential Enquiry into Maternal Deaths** — top causes: obstetric haemorrhage, hypertension, non-pregnancy-related infections (HIV/TB), sepsis.
- **CTOP Act** (Choice on Termination of Pregnancy).
- Notifiable: **maternal death** (statutory notification).

## Paediatrics
*Sources: Hospital Level (Paediatric) STGs & EML 5th ed 2023; IMCI (Integrated Management of Childhood Illness) [EK/WHO]; EPI-SA schedule (PHC Chapter 13 Immunisation v1.1 Dec 2024); Road to Health Booklet; WHO SAM guidelines; 2023 ART (paediatric/neonatal).*

### Non-negotiable history pillars — GENERAL PAEDIATRICS
- **Perinatal/birth history**: GA at birth, birth weight, mode of delivery, complications, resuscitation, SCBU/NICU stay.
- **Growth**: plot on **Road to Health Booklet (RTHB)** curves — weight-for-age, length/height, head circumference; growth trajectory.
- **Development/milestones** (gross motor, fine motor, language, social).
- **Immunisations** — up to date per **EPI-SA**? Check RTHB.
- **Feeding/nutrition** — breast/formula, weaning, current intake, appetite.
- **TB contact + HIV exposure/status** (mother's HIV, infant PCR results, PMTCT prophylaxis).
- **Hydration/output** in acute illness (wet nappies, fluid intake, vomiting/diarrhoea).
- Family/social: caregiver, socioeconomic, other siblings, deaths in children.

### Non-negotiable history pillars — NEONATAL
- Maternal antenatal history (HIV/VL/ART, syphilis, GBS, chorioamnionitis, PROM duration), GA, birth weight, APGAR, resuscitation, feeding, passage of meconium/urine, jaundice onset (hour of life).

### Non-negotiable history pillars — SAM (Severe Acute Malnutrition)
- Weight loss/failure to thrive, appetite, breastfeeding, weaning, recent illness/diarrhoea, HIV/TB, oedema, feeding practices, social (food security, caregiver).

### Examination routine (order actually performed)
1. **General/IMCI danger signs first**: not feeding/drinking, vomiting everything, convulsions, lethargic/unconscious.
2. **Anthropometry + plot**: weight, length/height, head circumference, MUAC (SAM), oedema check.
3. **Vitals + capillary refill + hydration** (age-appropriate ranges).
4. **Respiratory** — RR (age cut-offs), recession, nasal flaring, grunting, saturation, auscultation.
5. **CVS** — HR, pulses (femoral!), murmurs, hepatomegaly.
6. **Abdomen** — distension, tenderness, organomegaly.
7. **ENT/throat, fontanelle** (bulging/sunken), lymph nodes, skin (rash, turgor).
8. **Neuro** — tone, AVPU/GCS, meningism, primitive reflexes (neonate).
9. **Full exposure**: temperature, rash (meningococcus), NAI signs.

### Investigations TRACKED over time — and what the trend means
- **The weight curve IS the investigation** — serial weight on RTHB defines faltering vs catch-up growth; crossing centiles downward = red flag.
- **Head circumference trajectory** — hydrocephalus / microcephaly.
- **Serum bilirubin vs hour-of-life nomogram** (neonatal jaundice) — plot against age-in-hours + risk factors to decide phototherapy vs exchange transfusion.
- **Glucose** — neonatal/sick child hypoglycaemia (frequent monitoring).
- **Hydration/weight in gastroenteritis** — weight gain = rehydration success.
- **Hb** (anaemia, malaria), **CRP/WCC** in sepsis.
- **SAM monitoring**: weight (beware refeeding/oedema masking), electrolytes (K⁺, PO₄, Mg — **refeeding syndrome**), glucose, appetite test.
- **HIV**: infant PCR at birth/10 weeks; if positive → VL on ART.

### Classic omissions that cause harm
- **Not plotting growth** (RTHB) — the single most informative paediatric datapoint ignored.
- Not calculating **weight-based drug/fluid doses** (mg/kg, mL/kg) — dosing errors.
- Not checking **glucose** in the sick/convulsing/malnourished child.
- Missing **dehydration severity** grading (and giving wrong fluid plan A/B/C).
- **Refeeding syndrome** in SAM — feeding too fast/too much, not correcting electrolytes; SAM needs cautious F-75 then F-100, not standard fluids.
- Not palpating **femoral pulses** (coarctation) in neonate.
- Not screening **immunisation status** + catch-up.
- Missing **TB/HIV exposure** work-up.
- Missing **NAI**.
- Not recognising **neonatal jaundice** needing exchange (kernicterus).

### Scoring systems / charts to structurally support
- **Road to Health Booklet growth charts** (WFA, LFA/HFA, HC, WFH) + **MUAC**.
- **APGAR** (1 & 5 min).
- **Neonatal jaundice / bilirubin nomogram** (hour-specific).
- **IMCI classification** (danger signs, pneumonia/diarrhoea/fever traffic-light).
- **Paediatric SATS/TEWS** (age-banded vitals), **AVPU / paediatric GCS**.
- **Dehydration assessment (WHO plan A/B/C)**.
- **SAM 10 steps** (WHO), **Ballard/Dubowitz** (gestational age).
- **Westley croup score**, **PEWS**.

### SA-specific must-knows
- **EPI-SA schedule** (PHC Chapter 13, v1.1 Dec 2024): **Birth** — BCG + bOPV(0); **6 wks** — hexavalent (DTaP-IPV-HepB-Hib) 1, bOPV(1)/RV(1), PCV 1; **10 wks** — hexavalent 2; **14 wks** — hexavalent 3, RV 2, PCV 2; **6 & 12 months** — Measles/MR; **9 months** — PCV 3; **18 months** — hexavalent booster (DTaP-IPV-HepB-Hib) + Measles/MR; **6 yrs & 12 yrs** — Td; **9 yrs (girls)** — HPV. *(Note: PCV switched toward PCV10; MR replacing measles-only — confirm against local RTHB edition.)* **[partly EK — confirm dose products against current RTHB]**
- **PMTCT** (2023 ART): infant NVP ± AZT per risk; birth HIV PCR, repeat at 10 weeks + per algorithm; cotrimoxazole for HIV-exposed.
- **IMCI** is the district-level clinical algorithm.
- **SAM** managed per WHO 10 steps + SA guidelines (F-75/F-100/RUTF, cautious rehydration with ReSoMal).
- **Notifiable**: measles, acute flaccid paralysis (polio surveillance), pertussis, meningococcal, neonatal tetanus.

## Emergency Medicine
*Sources: South African Triage Scale (SATS) + TEWS (adult & paediatric); ATLS 10th ed [EK]; ACLS/AHA [EK]; Adult & Paediatric Hospital STGs & EML (emergencies).*

### Non-negotiable history pillars
- **AMPLE** (Allergies, Medications, Past history/Pregnancy, Last meal, Events/mechanism) — trauma standard.
- **Time of onset / mechanism / trajectory** of the presenting emergency.
- **Red-flag screen** for the complaint (e.g. chest pain → ACS/PE/dissection; headache → SAH/meningitis).
- **HIV/TB status**, **chronic meds + anticoagulants**, **last tetanus**.
- **Collateral** (from EMS, family) when patient can't give history.
- For overdose/poisoning: **substance, amount, time, co-ingestants, intent**.

### Examination routine (order actually performed)
1. **Triage first — SATS/TEWS** → colour priority.
2. **Primary survey (ABCDE)**: Airway + C-spine; Breathing (RR, sats, chest); Circulation (pulse, BP, bleeding control, IV access, bloods); Disability (**GCS/AVPU + pupils + glucose**); Exposure (temperature, full exposure, log-roll).
3. **Resuscitate as you find problems** — don't move on until each is addressed.
4. **Secondary survey** — head-to-toe, focused to complaint.
5. **Reassessment** after every intervention.

### Investigations TRACKED over time — and what the trend means
- **Serial vitals / TEWS** — trajectory drives escalation (a "yellow" trending to "orange").
- **Serial troponin** (0/1h or 0/3h) — ACS rule-in/out via delta.
- **Lactate + base deficit** — resuscitation adequacy / occult shock; clearance = improving.
- **Serial GCS** — deteriorating = intracranial catastrophe / airway threat.
- **Glucose** — repeat in hypoglycaemia/DKA management.
- **ABG/VBG + K⁺** in DKA, respiratory failure, toxidromes.
- **βhCG** — mandatory in reproductive-age abdominal pain/collapse.
- **FAST/eFAST** serial in trauma.

### Classic omissions that cause harm
- Not **triaging** / re-triaging the waiting patient → deterioration missed.
- Skipping **glucose** and **βhCG**.
- Anchoring on the first diagnosis; not doing a full **ABCDE**.
- Not controlling **catastrophic haemorrhage** first (C-ABC in trauma).
- Missing **time-critical windows**: STEMI (door-to-needle/balloon), stroke thrombolysis, sepsis antibiotics <1h.
- Inadequate **analgesia**.
- Not reassessing after intervention.
- Discharging without **safety-netting** / follow-up.
- Missing **occult shock** (normal BP, rising lactate/HR).

### Scoring systems / charts to structurally support
- **SATS + TEWS** (adult and paediatric, age-banded) — the SA triage backbone.
- **GCS** (component E/V/M), **AVPU**.
- **Wells (PE/DVT) + PERC**, **HEART / TIMI / GRACE** (chest pain), **CURB-65**.
- **NEXUS / Canadian C-spine**, **Ottawa** rules.
- **Parkland formula** (burns), **Rule of 9s**.
- **Poisoning toxidrome / paracetamol nomogram**.
- **NIHSS** (stroke), **CHA₂DS₂-VASc**.

### SA-specific must-knows
- **SATS** is the mandated national triage tool (Red/Orange/Yellow/Green/Blue + TEWS + discriminators).
- Extremely high **trauma/interpersonal-violence** load — **J88** documentation for assault/GSW/stab/GBV, chain of evidence.
- **HIV/TB** ubiquitous differentials (meningitis, PJP, TB, sepsis).
- **Rabies** post-exposure prophylaxis (dog bites — endemic areas), **snakebite** (polyvalent antivenom).
- **Notifiable**: rabies, cholera, meningococcal, measles, VHF, poisoning outbreaks.
- Sexual-assault care: PEP, emergency contraception, forensic (J88 + evidence kit).

## Intensive Care (ICU)
*Sources: Surviving Sepsis Campaign 2021 [EK]; ARDSNet / PROSEVA lung-protective ventilation [EK]; Adult Hospital STGs & EML (critical care); SASA/critical care practice [EK].*

### Non-negotiable history pillars
- **Reason for ICU admission + trajectory** and the **reversible pathology** being treated.
- **Premorbid functional status + comorbidities** (drives prognosis and ceiling-of-care).
- **Ceiling of care / resuscitation status** — explicitly established with family/team (scarce-resource context).
- **Full medication + allergy + substance** history (withdrawal risk).
- **HIV/TB status**, infection source + cultures + antibiotics + day of therapy.
- **Fluid balance + haemodynamic history** (pressors, response).
- **Ventilation history** (mode, settings, trajectory).

### Examination routine (order actually performed)
1. **A–E + monitor gestalt**: airway/tube position, ventilator (mode, settings, sats, ETCO₂), haemodynamics (BP/MAP, pressors, rhythm), sedation/GCS/RASS, temperature.
2. **Lines & tubes review** — ETT, CVC, arterial line, urinary catheter, drains, NG (sites, days in, infection).
3. **System-by-system daily review** (neuro → resp → CVS → renal/fluids → GIT/nutrition → haem/infection → skin/lines → prophylaxis).
4. **Focused exam**: chest (air entry, secretions), abdomen (feeding, distension), peripheries (perfusion, oedema, pressure areas).

### Investigations TRACKED over time — and what the trend means
- **Lactate clearance** — target falling trend; failure = ongoing shock/source-control failure.
- **P/F ratio (PaO₂/FiO₂)** — ARDS severity + response (mild 200–300, moderate 100–200, severe <100); guides PEEP/proning/paralysis.
- **ABG trend** — pH, pCO₂ (ventilation), HCO₃/base excess (metabolic); driving pressure + compliance.
- **UEC daily + urine output (mL/kg/h)** — AKI staging (KDIGO), RRT decision.
- **CRP/procalcitonin + WCC + temperature + cultures** — infection response / VAP.
- **FBC + coag (INR/aPTT/fibrinogen/platelets)** — DIC, bleeding, transfusion.
- **Fluid balance + weight** — cumulative positive balance = harm; de-resuscitation.
- **Glucose** (tight-ish control), **electrolytes incl. Mg/PO₄/Ca** (refeeding, arrhythmia).
- **Bilirubin/LFTs** (hepatic dysfunction), **CK** (rhabdo).

### Classic omissions that cause harm
- Not doing the **daily systematic review** (missing a developing complication).
- **Ventilator-associated harm** — not using lung-protective settings (Vt 6 mL/kg IBW, plateau <30, driving pressure <15).
- Not reviewing **line necessity** daily → CLABSI; missing **VAP** bundle.
- Over-sedation / no **sedation holds** or delirium screening (CAM-ICU).
- Missing **DVT + stress-ulcer prophylaxis** (FAST HUG mnemonic).
- Cumulative **fluid overload** — not de-resuscitating.
- Not chasing **source control** (drainable collection) while escalating antibiotics.
- Ignoring **electrolyte** derangements (K⁺, Mg, PO₄) → arrhythmia.
- No **nutrition** plan (early enteral).
- Not de-escalating **antibiotics** on cultures.

### Scoring systems / charts to structurally support
- **APACHE II / SOFA / qSOFA** (severity + trend).
- **GCS + RASS (sedation) + CAM-ICU (delirium)**.
- **P/F ratio + Berlin ARDS** criteria; **ventilator/observation chart**.
- **KDIGO AKI staging**.
- **FAST HUG** checklist (Feeding, Analgesia, Sedation, Thromboprophylaxis, Head-up, Ulcer prophylaxis, Glucose).
- **Fluid balance chart**, **Braden** (pressure), **CPOT** (pain).
- **NUTRIC** (nutrition risk).

### SA-specific must-knows
- **Bed rationing / triage of scarce ICU resources** — explicit admission criteria and ceiling-of-care decisions (esp. post-COVID frameworks).
- **HIV/TB**: PJP, TB (incl. TB-IRIS, disseminated TB, TB meningitis), cryptococcal meningitis — common ICU sepsis causes; ART timing decisions.
- **Trauma/post-op** dominate surgical ICU.
- **SASA/critical care** sedation & analgesia practice; limited RRT/ECMO access shapes decisions.

## Psychiatry
*Sources: Mental Health Care Act 17 of 2002 + regulations (MHCA forms); Adult Hospital STGs & EML (psychiatry); SASOP guidance [EK]; DSM-5-TR / ICD framework [EK].*

### Non-negotiable history pillars
- **Presenting complaint + timeline** of mood/psychotic/anxiety/cognitive symptoms.
- **Risk assessment — explicit**: suicidal ideation/plan/intent/means/past attempts; homicidal/violence risk; self-neglect; risk to dependents.
- **Substance use** (alcohol, cannabis, methamphetamine ["tik"], other) — quantify; withdrawal risk.
- **Psychiatric history**: previous episodes, admissions (voluntary/involuntary), treatments, response, adherence.
- **Full medical history + medications** — organic causes (thyroid, HIV, neurosyphilis, delirium, epilepsy, steroids).
- **HIV status** (HIV-associated neurocognitive disorder, CNS OIs).
- **Forensic/legal** history, **social** (housing, support, occupation, finances/grant), **collateral** (essential).
- **Developmental + family psychiatric** history.

### Examination routine (order actually performed)
1. **Mental State Examination (MSE)**: Appearance & behaviour → Speech → Mood & affect → Thought (form + content: delusions, suicidal/homicidal) → Perception (hallucinations) → Cognition (orientation, attention, memory; MMSE/MoCA) → Insight & judgement.
2. **Risk assessment** (structured — see above).
3. **Physical examination** — always, to exclude organic cause: neuro, vitals, signs of intoxication/withdrawal, thyroid, injection sites, injury.
4. **Cognitive screen** (MMSE/MoCA) where indicated.

### Investigations TRACKED over time — and what the trend means
- **Bloods to exclude organic cause**: TSH, glucose, U&E, calcium, FBC, LFTs, HIV, RPR/VDRL (neurosyphilis), B12/folate; CT brain / LP if first-episode psychosis/delirium/focal signs.
- **Therapeutic drug monitoring trends**: **lithium level** (0.6–0.8 mmol/L maintenance; toxic >1.2) with **U&E + TSH + Ca** periodically; **valproate**, **clozapine (FBC — neutropenia/agranulocytosis monitoring)**.
- **Metabolic monitoring on antipsychotics**: weight/BMI, waist, fasting glucose/HbA1c, lipids — trended (metabolic syndrome).
- **ECG/QTc** trend on QT-prolonging antipsychotics.
- **CIWA-Ar trend** in alcohol withdrawal; **withdrawal scales** for other substances.
- **Symptom rating trends** (PHQ-9, response over weeks).

### Classic omissions that cause harm
- **Not doing/ documenting a risk assessment** → preventable suicide/harm.
- Not excluding an **organic cause** (delirium mislabelled as "psych") — glucose, infection, hypoxia, drugs.
- Missing **substance intoxication/withdrawal** (esp. alcohol → seizures/DTs; needs thiamine + benzos).
- Not doing **baseline metabolic + ECG** before antipsychotics.
- Missing **lithium toxicity** (dehydration, NSAIDs, ACEi interaction).
- Not monitoring **clozapine FBC**.
- Not completing correct **MHCA forms** → unlawful detention.
- Overlooking **capacity** to consent.
- Neglecting **physical health** of the chronic psychiatric patient.

### Scoring systems / charts to structurally support
- **Mental State Examination** structure.
- **MMSE / MoCA** (cognition), **PHQ-9** (depression), **GAD-7** (anxiety), **EPDS** (perinatal).
- **CIWA-Ar** (alcohol withdrawal), **AUDIT** (alcohol use).
- **Columbia (C-SSRS)** / structured suicide risk.
- **AIMS** (tardive dyskinesia), **Glasgow Antipsychotic Side-effect Scale**.
- **HCR-20** (violence risk), **metabolic monitoring chart**.

### SA-specific must-knows
- **MHCA 17 of 2002** admission categories: **Voluntary**, **Assisted** (incapable of consent, does not refuse — MHCA-04 application, MHCA-05 assessments ×2), **Involuntary** (refuses, and risk to self/others/reputation — application MHCA-04, two independent MHCPs on **MHCA-05**, head of establishment decision **MHCA-07**, **72-hour assessment** outcome on **MHCA-08/09**, transfer **MHCA-11**), reviewed by **Mental Health Review Board**.
- **Section 40** — SAPS role in bringing mentally ill persons.
- **State patients / forensic** (accused persons — court referral, s77/78/79 observation).
- **HIV neuropsychiatric** disease common.
- **Methamphetamine ("tik")** and **cannabis** high prevalence.
- **Notify** as per regulations; document capacity + human-rights safeguards.

## Anaesthetics *(NEW department)*
*Sources: SASA (SA Society of Anaesthesiologists) Practice Guidelines / SAJAA [EK — SASA site not re-fetched this session]; WHO Surgical Safety Checklist; ASA physical status; difficult-airway/Mallampati conventions [EK]; SA malignant hyperthermia awareness [EK].*

### Non-negotiable history pillars
- **Previous anaesthetics + complications** — PONV, difficult intubation, awareness, prolonged recovery, dental damage, allergic reactions.
- **Family history of anaesthetic problems** — **malignant hyperthermia**, **suxamethonium (pseudocholinesterase) apnoea**.
- **Airway history/assessment** — snoring/OSA, previous difficult airway, neck movement, mouth opening, dentition.
- **ASA physical status class** (I–VI) + functional capacity (METs; can they climb 2 flights?).
- **Cardiorespiratory** — IHD, exercise tolerance, HTN control, asthma/COPD, recent URTI (esp. paeds).
- **Comorbidities affecting anaesthesia** — DM, reflux (aspiration), renal/hepatic, OSA, pregnancy.
- **Fasting/NPO status** (solids ≥6h, clear fluids ≥2h — "6-4-2" for solids/breastmilk/clear fluids in paeds).
- **Medications** — anticoagulants/antiplatelets (neuraxial timing), ACEi (hold), insulin/OHA plan, chronic opioids/benzos, herbals.
- **Allergies** (incl. latex, antibiotics, NSAIDs) + reaction type.
- **Substance use** (smoking, alcohol, recreational — tolerance/withdrawal).
- **Consent + planned procedure/site/side**.

### Examination routine (order actually performed)
1. **Airway assessment**: **Mallampati** (I–IV), thyromental distance (<6 cm concerning), mouth opening (<3 cm), neck extension, dentition/loose teeth, jaw protrusion, beard/obesity/OSA.
2. **Cardiovascular**: pulse, BP (both arms if relevant), murmurs, signs of failure, JVP.
3. **Respiratory**: RR, sats on air, auscultation, signs of URTI/wheeze.
4. **General**: BMI, venous access, frailty, dehydration, pregnancy.
5. **Site of regional/neuraxial** (spine, back landmarks, infection).
6. **Baseline neuro** if regional planned.

### Investigations TRACKED over time — and what the trend means
- **Intra-op continuous trends** — HR, BP/MAP, SpO₂, **ETCO₂** (ventilation + circuit integrity + MH early sign = rising ETCO₂), **temperature** (MH, hypothermia), airway pressures, agent concentration, neuromuscular monitoring (TOF).
- **Serial ABG** in long/major cases — acid-base, oxygenation, lactate.
- **Glucose** trend (diabetics intra-op).
- **Hb / blood loss** trend — transfusion trigger (~7 g/dL, higher if cardiac).
- **Urine output** (≥0.5 mL/kg/h) — perfusion.
- **Post-op recovery: Aldrete score trend** to discharge from PACU; **pain scores + sedation** trend.
- **Temperature recovery** and **NMB reversal (TOF ratio >0.9)**.

### Classic omissions that cause harm
- **Not assessing the airway** pre-op → unanticipated difficult intubation without a plan.
- Not confirming **fasting status** → aspiration.
- Missing **MH / sux apnoea family history** → catastrophic reaction (MH: dantrolene, cooling).
- Not stopping/timing **anticoagulants** before neuraxial → epidural haematoma.
- Not confirming **consent + site/side + WHO checklist**.
- **Under-monitoring** (no ETCO₂, no temperature in long cases).
- Not planning **postoperative analgesia** or PONV prophylaxis.
- Inadequate **reversal**/monitoring of NMB → residual paralysis, hypoventilation.
- Not recognising **MH** early (rising ETCO₂ + temperature + tachycardia + rigidity).
- Discharging from recovery before **Aldrete** criteria met.
- Not checking **glucose/K⁺** in diabetic/renal patients.

### Scoring systems / charts to structurally support
- **ASA physical status** (I–VI, + E for emergency).
- **Mallampati** + composite difficult-airway predictors (**LEMON**, thyromental distance).
- **WHO Surgical Safety Checklist** (shared with surgery).
- **Anaesthetic record chart** (continuous vitals, agents, fluids, drugs, events).
- **Aldrete / modified Aldrete recovery score** (PACU discharge, target ≥9).
- **Apfel score** (PONV risk).
- **STOP-BANG** (OSA), **RCRI/Lee** (cardiac risk), **NPO/fasting chart**.
- **Neuromuscular (TOF) + pain scores** (VAS/NRS).

### SA-specific must-knows
- **SASA practice guidelines** (monitoring standards, safe practice, sedation outside theatre) — align monitoring to SASA minimums (SpO₂, ECG, NIBP, capnography, temperature).
- High **HIV/TB** load — airway TB, drug interactions (rifampicin enzyme induction), immunosuppression.
- **Obstetric anaesthesia** burden (high C/S rate; spinal is workhorse; aspiration prophylaxis; failed intubation drill).
- **Resource variability** — dantrolene stock for MH, blood availability, limited HDU/ICU post-op beds influence case selection.
- **Trauma/emergency** high proportion — full-stomach/RSI default.
- Document consent + adverse events per medico-legal standards.

## Cross-cutting: the investigation-trending companion

The unifying app capability: **store every serial result as a time series, per patient per panel, and surface trajectory + rate-of-change + threshold breaches — not just the latest number.** "Thinking one step ahead of the intern" = predicting the next result the consultant will ask for and flagging the trend before it becomes a crisis.

### Core panels to trend across all disciplines
- **U&E/creatinine** — plot creatinine + eGFR + K⁺; compute Δ/24h. Prompt: *"Creatinine up 45% in 48h → AKI stage 1; review nephrotoxins on chart."* *"K⁺ rose 0.7 in 24h — patient on enalapril + spironolactone; get an ECG."*
- **FBC** — Hb (bleeding/haemolysis/marrow), WCC (sepsis response), platelets (sepsis/DIC/drug). Prompt on Hb drop ≥2 g/dL post-op or platelets falling in a PET/sepsis patient.
- **CRP ± ESR / procalcitonin** — falling = responding; **rising after post-op day 4 = complication**. Distinguish CRP-dominant (infection) vs ESR-dominant (inflammatory).
- **LFTs** — classify hepatocellular vs cholestatic; flag DILI on TB/ART; track bilirubin + INR (synthetic).
- **ABG/VBG** — pH/pCO₂/HCO₃/base excess/lactate; compute P/F ratio where FiO₂ known.
- **Glucose / HbA1c** — inpatient glucose titration trend + chronic control.
- **Coagulation** — INR (warfarin/liver), aPTT, fibrinogen (DIC/PPH).

### Per-discipline extras to trend
- **Internal medicine**: CD4/VL over months (suppression by 3–6 months); lithium/valproate/clozapine drug levels; troponin delta (0/1/3h); INR.
- **Surgery**: post-op Hb drift; **drain output (volume + character)**; CRP post-op day curve; NG/stoma losses; fluid balance.
- **Orthopaedics**: **serial neurovascular obs** (the alarm series); CK + renal function (rhabdo); CRP/ESR in bone/joint infection; post-op Hb.
- **O&G**: **βhCG doubling (48h, ≥66% rise)** for ectopic/molar; **BP + urine protein** for PET; **platelets/LFTs/LDH/creatinine** for HELLP; Hb after PPH; partogram cervimetry vs action line; serial CTG.
- **Paediatrics**: **weight curve on RTHB** (the primary series); head circumference; **bilirubin vs hour-of-life nomogram**; electrolytes (K⁺/PO₄/Mg) in SAM refeeding; hydration/weight in gastroenteritis.
- **Emergency**: serial TEWS/GCS; troponin delta; lactate clearance; repeat glucose.
- **ICU**: **lactate clearance**, **P/F ratio**, urine output mL/kg/h + KDIGO, cumulative fluid balance, procalcitonin/cultures + antibiotic day, TOF/RASS/CAM-ICU.
- **Psychiatry**: lithium level + U&E/TSH; clozapine FBC (neutrophils); antipsychotic metabolic panel + QTc; PHQ-9/CIWA trend.
- **Anaesthetics**: intra-op ETCO₂/temperature/BP/SpO₂ continuous; Aldrete recovery trend; Hb/blood loss vs transfusion trigger; TOF ratio recovery.

### Delta thresholds that should trigger a prompt (examples)
- **K⁺**: rise ≥0.5–0.7 mmol/L/24h, or any value ≥6.0 → ECG + treat; correlate to drug chart.
- **Creatinine**: rise ≥26.5 µmol/L in 48h or ≥1.5× baseline in 7 days → KDIGO AKI; check nephrotoxins.
- **Hb**: fall ≥2 g/dL, or <7 g/dL (transfusion consideration; higher trigger if cardiac).
- **Platelets**: falling trend in PET/sepsis; <100 in PET → HELLP work-up; <50 → bleeding risk.
- **CRP**: rising after post-op day 4 → source hunt.
- **Na⁺**: change >8–10 mmol/L/24h (osmotic demyelination risk / cerebral oedema).
- **Lactate**: failure to fall on serial measurement → escalate.
- **P/F ratio**: fall across 300/200/100 boundaries → escalate ventilation strategy (PEEP, proning, paralysis).
- **βhCG**: <66% rise over 48h → ectopic/non-viable pathway.
- **Bilirubin**: crossing phototherapy/exchange line on hour-specific nomogram.
- **Glucose**: <3.0 mmol/L (treat) or persistent >15 with ketones (DKA pathway).
- **QTc**: >500 ms on QT-prolonging drug → review.
- **Lithium**: >1.2 mmol/L → toxicity pathway.

### What "thinking one step ahead of the intern" concretely means per department
- **Medicine**: before the intern rounds, the app already lines up "creatinine trending up 3 days — dialysis criteria check?" and "CRP not falling on day 4 antibiotics — source?"
- **Surgery**: "drain output changed to bilious + rising CRP → suspect anastomotic leak" and "Hb down, HR up post-op → return-to-theatre conversation."
- **Orthopaedics**: "neurovascular obs deteriorating / pain escalating out of proportion → compartment syndrome — call now."
- **O&G**: "BP creeping + new proteinuria at 34 weeks → severe PET pathway, MgSO₄ + delivery planning"; "βhCG not doubling → ectopic protocol."
- **Paediatrics**: "weight crossing centiles down + poor feeding → faltering growth work-up"; "bilirubin approaching exchange line → escalate now."
- **Emergency**: "TEWS trending yellow→orange in the queue → re-triage"; "troponin delta positive → ACS pathway + timing."
- **ICU**: "lactate not clearing + rising pressor requirement → source control failure"; "P/F falling → prone/paralyse decision."
- **Psychiatry**: "lithium rising with a new NSAID/dehydration → toxicity"; "clozapine neutrophils dropping → stop + haematology."
- **Anaesthetics**: "rising ETCO₂ + temperature + tachycardia intra-op → malignant hyperthermia — dantrolene"; "Aldrete not reaching 9 → don't discharge from recovery."

### Design note
Every score/chart named above should be a **structured, reusable component** (fields, calculated total, interpretation band) rather than free text — this is what lets the app both prompt for missing inputs (e.g. a GCS with a missing motor component, a partogram without the action line plotted) and trend the outputs over time. Where SA guidelines specify the exact tool (SATS/TEWS, partogram, RTHB, MHCA forms, EPI schedule), those take precedence over generic global equivalents.
