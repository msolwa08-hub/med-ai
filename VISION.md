# MedAI — The Compass

> Read this first, every session. It is the goal. Everything else serves it.
> If a change does not make bedside reasoning **faster, clearer, or more
> certain**, flag it — do not build it. Motion is not progress.

## The soul

A **reasoning instrument you hold at the bedside.** Its one job: make your
**diagnostic uncertainty visible**, then hand you the **highest-yield next
move** to collapse it. The note and the plan fall out as byproducts. The
thinking is the product.

Universal — any practitioner, day or night: admission clerk, the 3am review,
the ward-round update. The intern leans on the teaching; the consultant leans
on the speed. Same instrument, **depth on demand.**

## The engine — one live scoreboard

3–4 differentials, each a **live %**. Fed by **four weighted evidence
streams**:

1. History
2. Exam
3. Investigation results
4. Response to treatment

Every move — a question, a sign, a test, a treatment — is scored by
**diagnostic yield** *and* **urgency/safety**. The app always surfaces the
single highest-yield next move, wherever it lives.

Each differential opens to a two-column ledger — **FOR | AGAINST** — and the
weighing produces the %. Unanswered discriminators appear as **Y/N taps**;
answering moves the %. A visible **certainty score you push upward.** Clerking
becomes an active game of collapsing uncertainty.

## The five tabs (clinical order)

1. **History · Exam · Differential** — you reason here. Discriminators surface
   at the **top**, never buried.
2. **Investigations** — ranked by diagnostic weight. Results close the loop.
3. **Management** — terse. Flags where treatment doubles as a diagnostic test
   (glucose wakes them → hypoglycaemia; salbutamol reverses → asthma; fluid
   lifts the pressure → empty tank). Treat the killer empirically when needed.
4. **Presentations / Documents** — the note / referral / SBAR it writes back.
5. **Explanation · Reasoning** — full words, on demand. The **only** place the
   narrative lives.

## The laws

- **Show, don't narrate.** Numbers, bars, weight, arrows — not sentences.
  Words live in exactly four places: **diagnosis names**, **the history you
  enter**, **presentations (tab 4)**, **the reasoning tab (tab 5)**. Everywhere
  else is near-wordless. Colourless, quick, glanceable.
- **Urgency is a marker, not a sentence.** A red flag on the row, not a
  paragraph.
- **Highest-yield next move.** Diagnostic weight + urgency/safety, always
  surfaced, always at the top.
- **Depth on demand.** The bedside stays wordless *because* tab 5 holds the why.
- **Compass over checklist.** See the top of this file.

## Contexts it must serve

- Bedside clerk on admission — the full loop.
- Quick review — the 3am look.
- Ward-round update — resume mid-loop: *what changed, what does it mean.*
